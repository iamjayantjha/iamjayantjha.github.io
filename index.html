JAI

Just Artificial IntelligenceJust Artificial Intelligence

2026

jai.zerostic.com (opens in new tab)

Zerostic's AI assistant. It runs inside Zerostic Studio and Admin.

Found

Clients do not read code commits, so real progress was invisible to them.

Clients came to rely on JAI every day, so an outage that took it offline was a real problem.

Decided

Let JAI read each GitHub commit and explain what was done in plain language.

Never depend on one AI provider. Run on Claude with Gemini as an automatic fallback.

Shipped

Plain-language progress updates, answers to client questions, and change requests filed as GitHub issues for the developers.

Estimates and proposals prepared from a client's idea.

Good Morning

Aug 2021 to present

Good Morning on Google Play (opens in new tab)

A free alarm and sleep app for Android and Wear OS, with no ads and no subscription. The watch reads heart rate and movement and rings the alarm when you are in light sleep.

Found

Interviews and surveys with 50+ students showed how they actually sleep and wake.

My first light-sleep detector scored worse than chance on real sleep data.

Decided

Replace it with a model trained on a public sleep dataset, and test every change by replaying recorded nights.

Add a wake-up check: after you dismiss the alarm, the watch keeps checking for about 40 minutes and wakes you again if you fall back asleep.

Shipped

Light-sleep wake-ups went from 63% to 75% on recorded nights.

1,000+ downloads and 4.7 stars on Google Play. Approved Samsung Health partner.

Zerostic Studio

Apr 2024 to present

studio.zerostic.com (opens in new tab)

A client portal where our clients follow their project from estimate to handover.

Found

We sent updates on WhatsApp and clients still called almost every day to ask about the status of their project.

Payments, invoices and call bookings were handled by hand, in different places.

Decided

Put everything a client needs in one portal, so they can check progress themselves instead of waiting for us.

Connect it to GitHub, so updates come from the actual work.

Shipped

Progress updates, messages, payments, invoices, a scheduler for booking calls and signed completion certificates in one place.

All our clients now use Studio, and status calls have almost stopped.

Zerostic Research

Sep 2024 to Jun 2025

research.zerostic.com (opens in new tab)

A user research tool built on what I studied in Design Thinking and User Research Methods at IIIT Hyderabad.

Found

Survey tools like Google Forms collect answers and stop there. With 1,000 responses, someone still has to work out who the users are and what they need.

Decided

Build the first diamond of the double diamond model into the product: understand, then analyse.

Start with one research method, surveys. Interviews come next.

Shipped

Survey responses turned into personas with pain points, gains and delighters, plus the insights behind them.
